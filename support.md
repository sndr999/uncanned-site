# Uncanned Support

Questions, problems or feedback? Email [Email support](mailto:unpouchedapp@gmail.com) and we'll get back to you.

**Cancel your subscription:** subscriptions are billed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Uncanned and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid the next charge.

**Delete your data:** in the app, open Settings (the gear on Today) and tap "Delete all my data". This removes everything from your phone and, if you have an account, its backup.

**Delete your account:** in the app, open Settings, then Account, and tap "Delete my account". Your account and its backup are removed from our servers.

If your subscription has ended, the subscription screen still has "Delete my data", and "Sign in" leads to your account, where "Delete my account" is.
